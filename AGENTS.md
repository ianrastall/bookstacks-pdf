# Bookstacks PDF publications

Follow ../AGENTS.md and ../_standards/. This repository owns derived PDF
publication files and their manifests. Canonical TEI, edition identities and
the reader website remain in ../bookstacks. Preserve edition language, source
credits, notes, artwork and existing URLs. PDFs are explicitly generated
offline; deployment validates and copies existing artifacts only. Every edition
has named light and dark variants with matching pagination and source hashes.
